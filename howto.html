How to Secure a Vehicle for Transport

Position Your Axle Straps

Begin by looping the axle straps around approved securement points, such as suitable axles or through the wheels. Make sure the straps are positioned securely and away from sharp edges, brake lines, or other components that could be damaged.

Connect Your Ratchet Straps

Attach the ratchet straps to the axle strap loops or hardware. Secure the opposite ends to the trailer's designated anchor points.

Tighten All Four Corners

Gradually tighten the ratchet straps at all four corners of the vehicle. Make sure each strap is properly tensioned and the vehicle is secure. Avoid overtightening.

Recheck After Departure

After approximately 5 minutes of driving, pull over in a safe location and inspect all four tie-down straps. Check for any movement or looseness and retighten as needed.

Safety Reminder

Always use properly rated straps and anchor points suitable for the vehicle's weight. Confirm that the vehicle is secured according to the manufacturer's recommendations before transporting.
