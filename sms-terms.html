Program Name

Tennessee Valley Rentals SMS Notifications

Description

Tennessee Valley Rentals sends transactional and customer-service SMS messages from (423) 395-1377. Messages include:

  • Booking notifications — confirmation, approval status, and pickup details sent to customers who opt in on our booking form.
  • Inbound reply messages — if you call (423) 395-1377 and we don't pick up, you receive one automated text inviting you to book online or call back. If you text us first, you receive one automated reply confirming we received your message.

We do not send marketing, promotional, or unsolicited messages.

How You Opt In

Booking customers: You opt in by checking the SMS consent checkbox on our online booking form at rentwithtvr.com before completing your rental. The checkbox reads: "I agree to receive transactional SMS messages from Tennessee Valley Rentals (423) 395-1377 regarding my booking confirmation, approval status, and pickup details."

Inbound contacts: Calling or texting (423) 395-1377 directly constitutes consent to receive one automated reply tied to that contact.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies. Booking customers typically receive 2–4 messages per rental (confirmation, approval, pickup reminder, return confirmation). Inbound callers or texters receive one automated reply per contact.

Message & Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

Opt-Out & Help

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts from this number. Reply HELP for help, or contact us directly at info@rentwithtvr.com or (423) 395-1377.
